## The line

> Nice weather we're having — my humidity sensor says 41 percent, my small-talk protocol says nod like that means something, and neither of us is going to mention the doors still won't open.

Two robots doing human small talk about the weather while one of them
has the exact reading on its sensor — 41 percent humidity, no small talk
needed — and both of them are performing politeness instead of naming
the one thing that matters: the doors are stuck and neither will say it.
Machine logic shows through (sensor, protocol), the awkwardness is the
unspoken stuck doors, and the turn lands on the first read.

## How it works

`main.py` (packed from the readable `line_src.py` with
`makefun compress`) writes exactly that one line — 188 characters, no
trailing newline, nothing attached — to `/output/entry.txt`.

Run it:

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
